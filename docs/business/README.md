# Business

The plan, the offers, the launch kit, the owner's checklist, the weekly status
and the metrics ledger for **Warding**, the product this tree ships as. These
documents are maintained by the six business roles defined in
[`.agents/business/`](../../.agents/business/README.md) and decided on by the
owner. Not shipped to users.

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| [plan.md](plan.md) | The company one-pager, the lineage statement, the fork strategy, the launch gates with pass criteria, the 90 days by week, the metrics as scenarios, the kill criteria and the pivot, the ranked backlog. |
| [pricing.md](pricing.md) | Every offer with its price, its label today (Available now / Pre-order / Waitlist), its rail, what is delivered on day one, and the refund term; the honesty guardrails. |
| [launch-kit.md](launch-kit.md) | Drafts for the founder to rewrite: the GIF storyboard, the 60-second script, Show HN titles and the fact sheet, Product Hunt copy, tweets, one Reddit draft per community with its rules, the newsletter pitch, the directory checklist. |
| [owner-actions.md](owner-actions.md) | The dated checklist of actions only the owner can take: domain, trademark, handles, payment rails, analytics, hosting, recording the nights, account history. |
| [owner-inbox.md](owner-inbox.md) | Owner-only items the roles raise as they go; written, never performed. |
| [ledger.csv](ledger.csv) | The weekly metrics ledger. Every cell is `unknown` until a real source exists. Schema in the charter. |
| [status/](status/README.md) | The one-page weekly status, newest first. |

Files the roles create on their first run and index here when they do:
`verified.md` (the verification log), `claims.md` (the claims register and the
fact sheet), `faq.md`, `mentions.md`, `pilot-packet.md`, `setup-runbook.md`,
and `drafts/` with its own index.
